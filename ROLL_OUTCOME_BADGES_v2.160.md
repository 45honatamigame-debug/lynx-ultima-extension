All two-die roll result surfaces now use one classification:
- FUMBLE: 1 + 1
- CRITICAL: existing critical rule (including Frenzy criticals)
- DOUBLE: matching dice that are neither Critical nor Fumble

Surfaces updated: local roll popup, global cinematic alert, Latest Result, Chat/Feed roll line, Study inline latest result, and Combat History.
