# v2.162 — Two-Weapon Fighting

- Added `TWO WEAPON` to Action Type for Player and Monster actions.
- Under Fabula Ultima Core Rulebook Two-Weapon Fighting, the High Roll (HR) is treated as 0 when calculating damage.
- The configured damage bonus (for example `+10`) is preserved. Example: a roll with High Roll 7 and Damage Bonus +10 deals 10 damage, not 17.
- Accuracy, critical/fumble/double detection, affinities, and monster level damage adjustments remain unchanged.
- Roll popup, background alert, feed, compact Action card and SEND text clearly display the HR=0 rule.
- This Action Type marks the attack profile as a Two-Weapon attack for HR calculation; it does not invent a second weapon profile or validate equipment categories.
