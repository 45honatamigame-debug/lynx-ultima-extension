# V3.0.61 — Centered Zero Power Cut-in + Tracker Fit

## Zero Power Release

- Opens in a large centered cinematic frame.
- Shows the linked GIF/image without replacing the player's saved data.
- Shows Charge/Trigger, Ability/Effect, and Description as separate sections.
- Keeps long content available through an internal scroll area.
- Remains open until the close button is pressed.
- Resets Zero Power from 6/6 to 0/6 after activation, as before.

## Initiative Tracker

- Places START, UNDO, NEXT, RESET, and HIDE in two stable rows.
- Reserves enough height for the conditional END TURN button.
- Keeps all HP, MP, and IP values inside compact combatant cards.
