# PLAYER NO TURN — v2.95

- Player cards in Scene now have the same GM-controlled NO TURN / JOIN TURN toggle as Allies.
- NO TURN players remain visible, targetable, editable, and token-spawnable in Scene.
- NO TURN players are excluded from Initiative, Next Turn, and round cycling.
- If the current Player is switched to NO TURN, combat advances to the next valid combatant immediately.
- The state persists with the player sheet across refresh/reconnect.
