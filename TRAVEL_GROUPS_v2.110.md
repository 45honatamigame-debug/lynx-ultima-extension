# TRAVEL GROUPS — v2.110

Flow:

1. Normal play uses MAIN Blank.
2. GM pre-positions A Blank and B Blank on the Owlbear MAP layer.
3. At a branching current Node, a player may right-click and split.
4. The initiator becomes Group B; other known members stay Group A. Both groups start on the same Node.
5. A and B move independently. Each move can advance only one linked Node.
6. A Blank follows Group A Current Node; B Blank follows Group B Current Node. The linked source Background remains untouched.
7. When both groups are on the same Node, GM may merge them; MAIN resumes from that Node.
