# V3.0.59 — Zero Power + Compact Combat HUD

- Manual six-step Zero Power fields live in the Player Sheet: name, trigger/charge condition, ability/effect, and description/GIF.
- The Zero Power canvas shortcut displays the owner's current charge at all times and highlights `FULL` at 6/6.
- Its popover lists party members for shared viewing, with charge adjustment restricted to the local owner.
- Clock hologram cards and their linked GIF previews are now substantially smaller.
- Initiative Tracker HP/MP/IP rows and character cards are more compact.
- Player sheet schema advanced to 12 with safe defaults for older sheets.
